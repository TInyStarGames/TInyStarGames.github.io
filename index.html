03 / THE STUDIO

Built around
exploration.

TinyStarGames is an independent game studio creating both original games and mods. We like building experiences that reward curiosity, discovery, progression, and the freedom to approach a world in your own way.

Our original development currently focuses primarily on Roblox, while our modding work gives us room to experiment with other games, systems, and communities.
